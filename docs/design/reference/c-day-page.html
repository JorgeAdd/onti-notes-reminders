Day Page

The app is a tear-off desk calendar. Every day is one page with big type, hours in the margin, and nothing else. Anything left unfinished yesterday stays as a torn strip of yesterday's page, stuck above today's.

ConceptOne page per day. The Today view is the product; everything else is the back of the pad.
TypeARCHIVO NARROW for days and headings, Source Serif 4 for notes, DM Mono for hours.
SignatureThe torn stub of yesterday's page. Reserved for one concept: carried-over (overdue) items.
Dark modeThe "night edition": a charcoal page on a black table, cream ink, softer green.

Rules

  • Accent: green belongs to the torn stub only. Today's page is ink on paper.
  • Motion: at the first open of a new day, yesterday's page tears off (320 ms: lift, slide up, fade), and only the stub stays behind. That's the one big motion in the app.
  • Motion: marking done draws the strike-through left to right (200 ms). Everything else is a 120 ms fade.
  • Reduced motion: no tear, no strike animation, no tilt on the stub. The new page simply appears.
  • Targets: every slot is at least 48 px tall and every button at least 44 px.
  • Keyboard: the shortcuts sit in the page footer, like the small print on a calendar page.
  • Density: medium. The big day name costs about 90 px, which suits a morning glance but is less ideal for long lists.
  • Risk: the All notes and filter views have no "day" to anchor them, so they need their own layout (here, a page titled with the tag).
