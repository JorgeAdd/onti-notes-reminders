Design direction A · reference sheet

Card Catalog

Every note is an index card on Jorge's desk. Cards that should have been done stay on the desk, a little crooked. Done cards get stamped and filed away. It's warm and tactile, and reads like a reference library more than a to-do app.

CONCEPT

Index cards on a desk. Notes are physical objects; the Today view is "what's on the desk right now".

TYPE

Newsreader for titles, IBM Plex Sans for UI, IBM Plex Mono for times, tags and shortcuts.

SIGNATURE

The FILED stamp, in vermilion. Reserved for one concept: completion. The accent never appears anywhere else.

DARK MODE

Dark cardstock on a walnut desk under a lamp. The ruled lines dim; the ink becomes cream.

Palette

Paper + ink + one accent. Toggle the theme to see the dark values.

Rules

  • Accent: vermilion is used only for the FILED stamp. Overdue is shown by position and tilt, never by red.
  • Motion: marking done stamps the card (120 ms scale 1.1→1) and then slides it down into the drawer (200 ms ease-out).
  • Motion: cards lift 2 px on hover and focus (160 ms). Nothing loops, nothing bounces.
  • Reduced motion: no tilt, no stamp scale, no slide. State changes are instant.
  • Targets: every button and card action is at least 44×44 px.
  • Keyboard: the main shortcuts are always visible in the top bar; ? lists the rest.
  • Density: cards keep the 24 px ruled baseline, which caps density at about 6 cards per screen on a laptop.
  • Risk: the most decorative of the three directions. Ruled lines must stay subtle or long notes get noisy.
